From shortlist to parcel

  1. Confirm the variant. Open the HipobuyFind listing, follow its seller link where available and record the color, size and price for your specific variant.
  2. Check agent eligibility. If you plan to use Sugargoo, submit the original seller URL in its order form and check whether that listing can be purchased and shipped to your destination.
  3. Review warehouse QC. Compare the item and measurements with your order. Check the applicable return window before approving shipment.
  4. Compare shipping quotes. Use the packed weight and dimensions when available. Compare chargeable weight, tracking and the quoted total for your destination.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Sugargoo spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list for shoppers comparing seller listings before purchasing through a shopping agent. This independent guide uses HipobuyFind listings as its catalog source.

What should I do if a listing has a price range?

Choose the exact size and color on the seller page. Use the price shown for that variant in your cost estimate and check whether the seller charges domestic postage.

How can I check the fit from a size chart?

For clothing, lay a similar garment flat and compare its chest width and length with measurements taken the same way. For shoes, compare insole lengths in centimeters.

Can I order every listed item through Sugargoo?

Check the original seller link with Sugargoo before paying. Order eligibility can depend on the listing, the available variant and your shipping destination.
